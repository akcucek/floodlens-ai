Gradient Boosting vs Naive Baseline

The naive baseline relies solely on rain thresholds (e.g. >75mm rain = impassable). FloodLens combines terrain elevation (DEM), GloFAS river discharge backflow, and road structural class to eliminate false negatives in low-lying sumps and false alarms on elevated expressways.
